# Dredge Queen — detail-opus5 self-report

`dredge-queen-detail-opus5.glb` · 33,124 tris (ceiling 45,000) · 1 material · 1 embedded 2048² atlas · 4 draw calls · byte-identical re-export under Blender 5.1.2 · base SHA `0edb3d7e`.

1. **I spent the budget on silhouette, because that is what the run camera resolves.** 2.8× the shipped triangles bought a lofted armoured hull with real sheer and tumblehome, a lattice derrick with chords/rungs/diagonals, 16-spoke paddlewheels with 18 bracketed blades, a ribbed wheelhouse dome, twin banded funnels, and a working deck — not finer tessellation of the shipped boxes.
2. **A lofted hull costs 188 triangles.** Twelve stations × twelve section points replaced the shipped hull's plain cube for less than 0.6% of the budget. Form was the cheapest thing I bought and the most visible; I would spend here first again.
3. **The claw is the boss, so I rebuilt it twice.** My first pass used beam-chains with knuckle spheres and rendered as a disc on spider legs. I added a parallel-transported `swept_tube` helper and re-cut the four talons as tapering curves that reach out and hook back under — fewer triangles than the beam chain, and it finally reads as a grab.
4. **I preserved every clause of the runtime's model guard except the one the duel raises.** Node names and order, `<node>Mesh` naming, one morph per mesh at index 0 with the shipped `Damage_*` names, identity node transforms, one material with a base-colour map (the runtime aliases it to `emissiveMap`), zero cameras/lights/animations.
5. **Drop-in cost is one integer.** `DredgeQueenBossSystem.ts:18` tests `triangles !== DREDGE_QUEEN_3D_TRIANGLES` — an equality test, not a ceiling — so adoption is `11_832 → 33_124` and nothing else. I did not edit `src/`; that is the judge's call, not mine.
6. **Bounds match the shipped body almost exactly**: 8.0 long by construction, 5.330 tall against the shipped 5.333. I tuned the rig height specifically so the model drops into the existing framing without a scale change; width came in narrower (4.52 vs 4.98) and that is the one dimension I did not land.
7. **Measured cost, not asserted cost.** Draw calls are unchanged at 4. Geometry grew 1.23→3.11 MB and morph storage 0.50→1.25 MB. The 2048 atlas is 16 MB of the 17.9 MB VRAM delta — **the detail is nearly free and the texture is the whole bill.** Shipping this at 1024 would recover ~16 MB and lose no geometry.
8. **Canon held.** Frontier-tech only, no firearms (ADR-001); the mainsail crest is two crossed *pickaxes* built as handle-plus-head, a tool not a weapon; teal is painted into the albedo and the material stays non-emissive; the Act-3 morphs stay silhouette events — sag, splay, split, flag struck — never gore.
9. **What I would do with more time, first:** the wheelhouse dome and loot-hold lids are still the two most abstracted surfaces, and the hold reads as a box with a lid rather than the plate's armoured bin. I would also widen the paddle sponsons to recover the missing 0.46 of beam.
10. **What I would do with more time, second:** run the blind gameplay-camera check *before* the turntable. I judged this model four times from a 45° review angle and only twice from anything like play distance — the craftbook warns about exactly that, and half my under-rim detail is unverified at the camera the player actually uses.
